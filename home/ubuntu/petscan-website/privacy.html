Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

PetScan AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the PetScan AI mobile application and website at petscan-ai.online.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication).
  • Pet information: Pet names, species, breed, age, weight, and profile photos you provide.
  • Scan data: Photos you upload for health scans and the AI-generated results.
  • Usage data: App interactions, feature usage, and device information for improving the service.
  • Payment information: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered pet health scan results and wellness recommendations.
  • To maintain your account and pet profiles.
  • To process premium subscription purchases.
  • To improve the accuracy and quality of our AI models.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g., email verification, password reset).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and database storage.
  • OpenAI: AI analysis of pet photos and chat responses. Photos are sent to OpenAI's API and are subject to OpenAI's data usage policies.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and purchase verification.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from the app's Settings screen, which will permanently remove your data from our systems.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), Firebase Authentication, and secure cloud storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

PetScan AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@petscan-ai.online.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@petscan-ai.online.
